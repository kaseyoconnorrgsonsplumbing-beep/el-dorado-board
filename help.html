Getting Started on the Room Board

How to create your login and start checking off work — takes about a minute.

The El Dorado room board lets you see every room, check off the work you finish, and add photos and notes right from your phone. Everything you complete is stamped with your name.

Before you can check off work, you set up a quick login. You only do this once.

Step 1 · Open the board
1
Open the board on your phone

Either scan the QR code sticker on the front of a room — it opens that room automatically — or open the link the office sent you (it's at the bottom of this sheet). Add it to your home screen if you want it handy.

Step 2 · Create your login
2
Tap Log in

Tap Log in in the top-right corner. If you opened a room, you can also tap the Log in button on the "viewing as a guest" bar.

3
Tap "Create your login"

On the Sign in screen, look under "New to the crew?" and tap Create your login. (The top box is only for people who already made a login.)

4
Fill in your details

Enter:

• Your name (first and last)
• Company (e.g. R.G. & Sons, or your sub)
• Create a password (at least 4 characters)

Tap Show next to the password to see what you typed so you don't mistype it. It's totally fine to use the same password as a coworker.

5
Tap Create login & start

Tap Create login & start. You'll see a "You're in" message — and that's it.

You can start working immediately

You don't have to wait for approval. As soon as you request access you can check off tasks, add photos, and leave notes — everything saves and is credited to you. The office confirms your login shortly after; nothing you do in the meantime is lost.

After that — every day

Next time you open the board, just tap Log in, enter your password, and tap Sign in. You stay logged in until you log out, so most days you won't even need to.

Every box you check and every photo you add shows your name, so the office always knows who did what.

If something doesn't work
It asks "Who's signing in?"
That just means a coworker uses the same password as you. Tap your name from the list and you're in.
"We didn't find that password"
Double-check what you typed (tap Show to see it). If you've never made a login, tap "Create your login" instead.
I forgot my password
The office can look it up or reset it for you from the Config screen — just ask.
The board says "Local only" or won't load
Check your signal. The app keeps working offline and syncs when you're back in range, so keep going — your work is saved on your phone in the meantime.
